# Sarinda Restaurant — Design System
## Design Concept 2: Fresh & Vibrant

### Design Direction
Friendly, energetic, appetizing and contemporary. The design should communicate freshness and family-friendly dining rather than luxury fine dining.

### Visual Personality
- Fresh
- Warm
- Social
- Appetizing
- Trustworthy
- Easy to use

### Color System
- Primary: Deep restaurant green
- Secondary: Fresh leaf green
- Accent: Warm orange/yellow inspired by food
- Background: Warm off-white
- Surface: White
- Text: Deep charcoal/green
- Success: Natural green
- Error: Warm red

Use the accent color sparingly for offers, badges and important food highlights.

### Typography
- Headings: Elegant modern serif or high-quality display serif
- Body/UI: Clean sans-serif
- Large, bold hero headline
- Short paragraphs and highly scannable labels

### Layout
- 12-column desktop grid
- Maximum content width around 1200–1280px
- Generous white space
- Rounded cards
- Soft shadows
- Large food photography
- Strong CTA hierarchy

### Homepage Structure
1. Header
2. Hero
3. Trust/value points
4. Popular menu
5. Signature dish
6. Offers
7. About Sarinda
8. Reviews
9. Gallery
10. Reservation/order CTA
11. Location
12. Footer

### Header
Logo left. Navigation: Home, Menu, Offers, Gallery, About, Contact. Right side: search icon + prominent Order Now button.

### Hero
Headline example: “Good Food Brings People Together”
Supporting copy should be short and emotional.
Primary CTA: Order Now
Secondary CTA: Book a Table
Hero visual: high-quality biryani/hero dish with a natural food composition.

### Food Cards
Each card contains:
- Food image
- Category/item name
- Short descriptor
- Price
- Optional popular badge
- Add button

### Offers
Use colorful but controlled promotional cards. Every offer should have a single clear CTA.

### Imagery
Prioritize authentic food photography:
- Natural lighting
- Visible texture
- Close-up dishes
- Consistent aspect ratios
- Avoid generic stock imagery

### Responsive Rules
Mobile navigation becomes a compact menu.
Hero stacks text above image.
Menu becomes 2-column or 1-column cards depending on width.
Sticky mobile bottom bar: Menu / Cart / Order.

### Interaction
- Buttons have clear hover/pressed states.
- Cards lift subtly on hover.
- Add-to-cart gives immediate feedback.
- Filters remain easy to access on mobile.
- Avoid excessive animation; food remains the visual focus.

### Brand Rule
The website should look distinctly like a real restaurant brand, not a generic SaaS template.
