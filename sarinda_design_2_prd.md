# Sarinda Restaurant — PRD
## Design Concept 2: Fresh & Vibrant

### 1. Product Overview
A modern restaurant website and digital ordering platform for Sarinda Restaurant. The experience should feel fresh, friendly, appetizing, and trustworthy while converting visitors from Facebook, Instagram, Google, and QR codes into orders, reservations, and repeat customers.

### 2. Goals
- Establish a strong independent digital presence.
- Increase direct online orders and reduce dependency on third-party marketplaces.
- Make the menu fast and easy to browse.
- Increase table reservations and event inquiries.
- Capture first-party customer data with consent.
- Create a foundation for AI automation and Facebook/Instagram marketing.
- Track measurable conversions from campaigns.

### 3. Primary Users
- Local customers looking for lunch/dinner.
- Families and groups.
- Existing customers coming from Facebook/Instagram.
- Delivery customers.
- Customers scanning a restaurant QR code.
- Birthday/event customers.
- Restaurant staff and owners managing orders/leads.

### 4. MVP Scope
#### Customer-facing
- Homepage
- Digital menu
- Category browsing
- Menu item details
- Cart
- Direct ordering
- Cash on delivery / configurable digital payment
- Table reservation
- Offers/promotions
- Gallery
- About/location/contact
- Click-to-call and WhatsApp/Messenger CTA
- Mobile-first responsive design
- SEO-ready pages

#### Admin
- Menu/category/item management
- Price and availability management
- Order management
- Reservation management
- Offer management
- Basic customer/order history
- Dashboard with orders, revenue and popular items

### 5. Growth & Automation Scope
- Meta Pixel and conversion events
- Google Analytics/Tag Manager-ready tracking
- Facebook/Instagram campaign landing pages
- AI FAQ assistant for menu, location, hours, offers and reservation questions
- WhatsApp/Messenger automation
- Automated order confirmation
- Review request automation
- Customer segmentation for future campaigns
- Retargeting audiences based on consent and platform capabilities

### 6. Key User Flows
#### Order
Ad/Social → Landing Page → Menu → Item → Cart → Checkout → Confirmation

#### Reservation
Homepage → Book a Table → Date/Time/Guests → Contact → Confirmation

#### AI
Facebook/WhatsApp → Customer question → AI answer → Menu/offer link → Order or reservation

#### Marketing
Facebook Ad → Campaign landing page → Offer → Order → Conversion event → Retargeting

### 7. Success Metrics
- Direct orders per month
- Conversion rate
- Average order value
- Reservation requests
- Event leads
- Repeat customer rate
- Website-to-order conversion
- Ad-to-order conversion
- Cost per lead/order
- AI automation resolution rate

### 8. Non-Functional Requirements
- Mobile-first
- Fast page load
- Secure checkout and forms
- Accessible typography and contrast
- SEO-friendly semantic structure
- CMS/admin-ready architecture
- Analytics events documented
- Bangla/English content capability

### 9. Future Phase

- Personalized recommendations

- Advanced CRM
- AI sales assistant
